---

iDEA is a student-led innovation club at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore, focused on transforming ideas into working projects, impactful solutions, and viable products. It brings together students interested in technology, research, software development, and real-world problem-solving through hands-on engineering and collaboration. Through project teams and Special Interest Groups (SIGs), members explore technical domains, experiment with emerging technologies, and develop solutions that progress from initial concepts to prototypes and deployed systems.

At iDEA, innovation goes beyond generating ideas. Members identify problems, research existing approaches, validate assumptions, define project requirements, and build solutions through structured engineering and continuous iteration. The club encourages technical depth, collaborative development, and research-driven experimentation while helping members develop the skills required to take projects from concept to practical implementation. Promising projects can progress toward industry collaboration, external partnerships, funding opportunities, incubation, and startup development, connecting technical innovation with practical application and entrepreneurship.

iDEA's legacy extends beyond its ongoing projects through an alumni network whose members have pursued careers across leading companies, contributed to diverse technical fields, and built their own ventures. Their journeys reflect the range of opportunities available across industry, research, engineering, and entrepreneurship. Together, the achievements of former ideators represent iDEA's continued impact and the possibilities it seeks to create for future generations of innovators.
